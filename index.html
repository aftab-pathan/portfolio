about me

Turning complex systems into elegant interfaces

With 5+ years of production experience, I build scalable web applications across diverse domains — financial SaaS, enterprise fleet management, aviation fuel operations, and developer tooling. My work spans pixel-perfect React UIs to full-stack Next.js products with Node.js/Express backends.

I thrive in complex, data-heavy environments — architecting multi-role dashboards, integrating real-time WebSocket data feeds, visualizing large-scale datasets with AG Grid and Leaflet maps, and collaborating closely with product and design to deliver polished, production-ready interfaces.

At Simform, I led frontend architecture for a financial SaaS platform. At Infosys, I delivered enterprise solutions for global clients in the EV mobility and aviation sectors. Each engagement demanded different domain knowledge — and I brought the same engineering rigour to all of them.

Outside work I ship personal projects: AI-powered tools, a resume builder on Vercel, a browser code IDE, and algorithmic trading experiments. I learn by building across stacks.
